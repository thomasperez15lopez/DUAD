   1.Verify the environment
     1.Confirm you are at home.
     2.Confirm a computer is available.

   2 .Verify system readiness
     1.Ensure the computer is powered on.

   3.Verify required tools
     1.Confirm a TV remote control is available.

2. Turn On the TV
  1.Locate the remote control
     1.Search the table or nearby furniture.
     2.Identify the remote that belongs to the TV.
  2.Pick up the remote control
     1.Grab the remote with one hand.
     2.Ensure the front of the remote faces outward.
  3.Point the remote toward the TV
     1.Face the television.
     2.Aim the remote toward the TV sensor.
  4.Locate the power button
     1.Look at the top section of the remote.
     2.Identify the red circular button labeled Power.
  5.Press the power button
     1.Press the button once with your finger.
     2.Release the button.
  6.Confirm the TV is on
     1.Observe that the screen lights up.
     2.Check that the standby light disappears.
     3.Wait until the TV finishes loading the display.
3. Access a Web Browser
  1.Locate the browser application
     1.Move the mouse cursor to the desktop.
     2.Look for the Firefox application icon.
  2.Open Firefox
     1.Move the mouse cursor over the Firefox icon.
     2.Launch the browser
       1.Double-click the left mouse button.
       2.Wait for the application to open.

  1.Navigate to the browser address bar

     2.Click once inside the address bar.   
  2.Prepare the address bar
     1.Highlight any existing text
	   1.Drag the cursor across the text.
     2.Delete existing text
	   1.Press the Delete key on the keyboard.
  3.Enter the website address
     1.Type youtube.com
	   1.Press the corresponding keys on the keyboard.

6.Search for a Video
  1.Locate the YouTube search bar
     1.Look at the top center of the webpage.	
     2.Identify the rectangular search field.
  2.Activate the search bar
     1.Move the mouse cursor over the search field.
     2.Click once inside the field.
  3.Enter the video title
     1.Type the name of the desired video
       1.Press the appropriate keys on the keyboard.
       2.Verify the typed text appears correctly.
  4.Execute the search
     1.Press the Enter key. 
     2.Wait for the search results page to load.
7.Select a Video
  1.Review the search results
     1.Observe the list of videos displayed.
     2.Identify the desired video by title or thumbnail.
  2.Scroll if necessary
     1.Use the mouse scroll wheel.
     2.Continue scrolling until the desired video appears.
  3.Open the video
     1.Move the mouse cursor over the video thumbnail.
     2.Click the left mouse button once.
8.Play the Video
  1.Wait for the video page to load
     1.Observe the YouTube video player.
     2.Wait for buffering to complete.	 
  2.Start the video if autoplay is disabled.
     1.Locate the play button
	   1.Move the mouse over the video player.
     2.Click the play button.

    1.Wait until the video finishes Or manually stop the playback.
  2.Close the browser
    1.Move the cursor to the top right corner of the window.
    2.Click the X button.
